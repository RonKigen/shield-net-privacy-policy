Privacy Policy

App Name: ShieldNet: No Root Blocker | Last updated: November 9, 2026

At ShieldNet: No Root Blocker ("we", "our", or "us"), we prioritize your digital privacy above all else. This Privacy Policy outlines how ShieldNet handles information when you use our application.

Our Core Privacy Promise

ShieldNet does not collect, store, log, or transmit any of your personal data or browsing traffic to external servers. The app operates 100% locally on your device.

1. How the App Works (Android VpnService)

ShieldNet uses the Android VpnService framework solely to create a local, on-device virtual private network interface. This interface allows the app to intercept, filter, and block network packets locally on your phone based on the rules you set.

  • Your internet traffic is never routed through a remote proxy, commercial VPN server, or third-party relay.
  • No data packet contents are read, saved, or uploaded.

2. Data Collection

We do not collect any personal information, usage analytics, diagnostic data, or telemetry. Specifically:

  • No Tracking: We do not use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No Advertising: The app contains no ads and shares no data with advertisers.
  • No "Calling Home": ShieldNet does not communicate with any backend servers owned or operated by us.

3. Permissions Required

ShieldNet requests specific system permissions strictly to function as a local firewall:

  • BIND_VPN_SERVICE: Essential to run the local packet filtering loop on your device.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE: Keeps the local filtering service active in the background so your app rules remain enforced.
  • QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES / Package Visibility: Allows you to view a list of installed applications so you can individually select which ones are allowed or blocked from accessing the internet.

4. Security

Because all network rules, logs (if enabled locally for debugging), and configurations are kept strictly on your local device storage, your data remains secure under your device's built-in operating system security measures.
